The Wetherspoons menu is popular with people looking for familiar pub meals, breakfast options, international flavours, and food-and-drink deals in the UK. Whether you are planning an early breakfast, a relaxed lunch, or an evening meal with friends, the menu offers a variety of dishes to suit different appetites.

J D Wetherspoon combines traditional British pub favourites with modern choices, including burgers, pizzas, curries, small plates, and meat-free meals. Many dishes are also available as meal deals that include a selected drink, making it easier for customers to compare options before ordering.

In this guide, you will discover the main Wetherspoons menu categories, breakfast choices, popular meals, weekly promotions, dietary information, ordering methods, and useful tips for checking local prices.

What Can You Find on the Wetherspoons Menu?

Wetherspoons offers a broad selection of food and drinks, although the precise dishes and prices can vary by pub. The menu is designed to accommodate different occasions, from quick snacks to substantial cooked meals.

The main categories commonly include:

Wetherspoons Breakfast Menu

Breakfast is one of the chain's best-known offerings. The standard breakfast menu is served from 8 am until noon, seven days a week, although customers should check their local pub for any variations. Hotel breakfast service may begin earlier.

Traditional breakfast options generally include eggs, bacon, sausages, baked beans, hash browns, mushrooms, tomatoes, and toast. Different portion sizes allow customers to choose a lighter meal or a more filling plate.

Other breakfast choices may include breakfast muffins, sausage or bacon sandwiches, vegetarian breakfasts, porridge, scrambled eggs on toast, and fruit-based options. Tea, coffee, and hot chocolate are also available, with free refills on tea and coffee subject to the menu's terms.

For anyone visiting before work or planning a relaxed weekend morning, the breakfast selection provides alternatives to a full cooked meal.

Burgers and Chicken Meals

Burgers are another important part of the Wetherspoons menu. Customers can explore traditional beef burgers, gourmet-style combinations, and chicken options, depending on the pub's current selection.

A classic burger can suit someone looking for a familiar pub meal, while a more elaborate burger may include additional toppings, sauces, or cheese. Chicken dishes provide another choice for customers who prefer poultry to beef.

The menu also includes meat-free burger options in its wider selection. Customers with specific dietary requirements should review the current ingredients and allergen information before ordering.

🔎

When comparing burger meals, check whether the displayed price includes a drink and whether sides are included. This makes it easier to compare the total cost of different options.

Fish and Chips: A British Pub Favourite

Fish and chips remains a familiar choice for customers who enjoy traditional British food. The dish typically combines battered fish with chips and may be served with additional accompaniments according to the menu.

Wetherspoon highlights cod and haddock in its fish-and-chips offering. The company's official food information also discusses sourcing from fisheries certified as well-managed and sustainable.

Fish and chips can be suitable for lunch or dinner, especially for customers who prefer a classic pub dish over burgers or international meals.

Before ordering, check the menu at your chosen pub for the available fish option, serving details, price, and allergen information.

Curries and International Dishes

The Wetherspoons menu extends beyond traditional British pub food. Curry dishes provide an alternative for customers who enjoy aromatic sauces, spices, rice, and other familiar accompaniments.

The selection can vary, but curry options are an important part of the chain's weekly promotions. Customers who enjoy stronger flavours may prefer a curry, while those looking for something lighter can compare other available dishes.

The wider menu has also featured international-inspired options such as noodle bowls and shawarma-style wraps. However, individual dishes may change over time, so the current menu at the local pub is the best reference.

Pizza and Small Plates

Pizza is a convenient option for people who want a familiar meal without choosing a traditional pub classic. Wetherspoon advertises 11-inch pizzas and also incorporates pizzas into certain meal and small-plate offers.

Small plates are useful for customers who want to try different flavours or share several items. Depending on the current selection, these can include snack-sized dishes, sides, and other savoury options.

This category works particularly well for groups who prefer ordering a variety of items rather than choosing one large meal each.

Wetherspoons Menu Prices

Prices depend on the individual pub, location, and current menu. Airport, railway-station, and other high-cost locations may differ from ordinary high-street branches. Older menus and third-party websites can therefore be useful for reference, but they should not be treated as a guarantee of today's price.

Vegetarian and Vegan Options

Customers who do not eat meat can look for vegetarian and vegan choices on the Wetherspoons menu. Depending on the current selection, these may include plant-based sausages, vegan breakfasts, meat-free burgers, and other suitable dishes.

Menu symbols can help identify dietary categories, while the official website, app, and in-pub information screens provide further ingredient and nutritional details.

It is important to distinguish between a dish being labelled vegetarian or vegan and a kitchen guaranteeing complete separation from other foods. Wetherspoon states that shared preparation or cooking areas may be used, so it cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment.

⚠️

Anyone with a food allergy should consult the latest published allergen information and speak to staff before ordering.

How to Order from the Wetherspoons Menu

Wetherspoon offers different ways to order food and drinks. Customers can use the official app or scan a table QR code at participating pubs. Ordering at the bar is another option.

The Wetherspoons app can also help customers find pubs and access relevant menu information.

A simple ordering process is:

  1. Choose the Wetherspoon pub you plan to visit.
  2. Check its current food and drinks menu.
  3. Review the price, ingredients, and any deal conditions.
  4. Note your table number if ordering through the app or QR code.
  5. Confirm your order and payment details before submitting.

Checking the local menu first is particularly helpful when you are visiting an unfamiliar branch or looking for a specific dish.

Tips for Choosing the Right Meal

The best Wetherspoons meal depends on your appetite, dietary needs, schedule, and budget. A breakfast muffin or toast may suit someone in a hurry, whereas a cooked breakfast is better for a more substantial morning meal.

At lunchtime, customers can compare burgers, fish and chips, pizzas, and lighter dishes. Groups may prefer small plates because they allow everyone to choose different items. Weekly promotions can also be worth considering if the featured category matches your preferences.

If calories or allergens are important to you, use the current official menu and nutritional information rather than relying on an older online article. The same principle applies to prices: confirm them with the branch you intend to visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is included on the Wetherspoons menu?
The menu features breakfast, burgers, chicken dishes, fish and chips, curries, pizzas, small plates, lighter meals, vegetarian and vegan options, desserts, and drinks. Exact choices vary by pub.
2. What time does Wetherspoons serve breakfast?
The standard breakfast menu is served from 8 am until noon, seven days a week. Hotel breakfast service can begin at 7 am. Check the local pub for specific arrangements.
3. Does Wetherspoons offer vegetarian and vegan food?
Yes. Vegetarian and vegan options are available, although the selection varies. Check the current menu symbols and ingredient information before ordering.
4. Does the Wetherspoons menu include drinks with meals?
Many menu offers include a qualifying drink, while other dishes and drinks are sold separately. Always read the conditions attached to the specific meal or promotion.
5. Are Wetherspoons menu prices the same everywhere?
No. Prices may vary by pub and location. Check the menu for your chosen branch to confirm the current amount.
6. What are the Wetherspoons weekly deals?
The regular club-deal schedule includes small plates on Monday, gourmet burgers on Tuesday, pizza on Wednesday, and Curry Club on Thursday. Afternoon deals are also advertised for weekdays. Local availability and conditions may vary.
7. Can I order through the Wetherspoons app?
Yes. The official app supports food and drink ordering at participating pubs. Customers can also use table QR codes or order at the bar.
8. Where can I find allergen information?
Allergen and nutritional information is available through the official Wetherspoon website, app, and in-pub customer information screens. Speak to staff if you have a specific allergy. The company cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment.
9. Does Wetherspoons offer children's meals?
Yes. Children's meals are included in the company's menu offering. Check the local menu for current choices, prices, and meal details.

Conclusion

The Wetherspoons menu offers a broad choice of food and drinks, making it a useful option for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and casual meet-ups. From traditional cooked breakfasts and fish and chips to burgers, curries, pizzas, and meat-free meals, customers can compare different dishes according to their tastes and needs.

Weekly club deals add another reason to check the menu before visiting, while the official app and website make it easier to review local options and ordering information.

For the best experience, confirm current prices, promotion details, opening arrangements, and allergen information with your chosen pub. This helps you select a meal that fits your preferences and enjoy your visit with greater confidence.
